# Summary

## Status (2026-07-15)

GCC-PHAT → TDOA → angle pipeline implemented and verified against
pyroomacoustics simulations. Live-demo hardware check done.

## Simulation results

Setup: 2 mics, 0.15 m spacing, source 2.0 m away, fs = 16 kHz, 0.5 s white
noise burst, angles swept −80°…+80° in 10° steps (17 angles per scenario),
GCC-PHAT with 16× interpolation. Seed fixed (42), fully reproducible via
`.venv/bin/python src/run_sim.py`.

| Scenario | Room | Noise | Mean \|error\| | Max \|error\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anechoic | 6×5 m, no reflections | none | 0.20° | 0.72° |
| Reverberant | 6×5 m, RT60 = 0.3 s | 15 dB SNR | 0.57° | 1.23° |

Errors grow toward endfire (±80°) as expected — `arcsin` sensitivity to
τ error increases near ±90°.

Per-angle details in `results/sim_results.csv`; plot in
`plots/angle_sweep.png`. Geometry diagram (room, mic array, source arc):
`plots/room_geometry.png`, generated by `src/plot_geometry.py`.
Single-case pipeline walkthrough at +40° (input waveforms → GCC-PHAT peak →
arcsin): `plots/pipeline_plus40.png`, generated by `src/plot_pipeline.py`
(measured τ = 281.2 µs vs geometric 281.1 µs → 40.03°).

## Delay-and-sum beamformer

`src/beamformer.py` adds a steered-response-power DOA estimator: delay one
channel per candidate angle (FFT fractional delay), sum, pick the angle with
max output power (0.5° scan). For the +40° source, the peak lands at +40.0°
in both anechoic and reverberant conditions (`plots/beamformer_plus40.png`,
via `src/plot_beamformer.py`). The reverberant curve sits on a higher power
floor — reflected energy arrives from all directions — but the main lobe is
unmoved, consistent with the GCC-PHAT results.

## MVDR (Capon) beamformer

`src/mvdr.py` computes a broadband MVDR pseudo-spectrum (STFT, per-bin 2×2
covariance with 1% diagonal loading, `1/(aᴴR⁻¹a)` averaged over 300–7000 Hz,
0.5° scan). Results for the +40° source (`plots/mvdr_plus40.png`, via
`src/plot_mvdr.py`):

| Method | Anechoic peak | Reverberant peak | Sidelobe floor (anechoic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MVDR | +40.0° | +39.5° | ≈ −11 dB |
| Delay-and-sum | +40.0° | +40.0° | ≈ −3 dB |

MVDR's main lobe is far sharper (~8 dB more sidelobe suppression) in clean
conditions; under RT60 = 0.3 s reverb the covariance is polluted by
reflections and the MVDR spectrum flattens toward the delay-and-sum shape,
with the peak still within 0.5° of truth.

Stage-by-stage walkthrough: `plots/mvdr_pipeline_plus40.png` (via
`src/plot_mvdr_pipeline.py`) — STFT, measured cross-covariance phase
arg R₀₁(f) matching the theoretical −2πf·τ slope exactly, per-bin Capon
spectra (vertical ridge at +40°; curved spatial-aliasing ghosts above
~1.5 kHz that the band average suppresses), final spectrum → +40.0°.

All three DOA methods (GCC-PHAT, delay-and-sum, MVDR) independently agree
at the +40° test case.

## Hardware verification (MacBook Pro 14, M5)

- Python capture via `sounddevice` works (48 kHz, verified live recording).
- Built-in mic exposes **1 channel only** (macOS beamforms the 3-mic array),
  so the live 2-mic demo needs an external 2-channel USB device.
  Aggregate device (MacBook + iPhone mics) is not viable: independent clocks
  drift far more than the ~µs timing the TDOA needs.

## Decisions

- Angle convention: 0° = broadside, positive toward the +x mic; range ±90°.
- 16× IFFT interpolation → ~0.5° resolution near broadside at this geometry.
- Verification-first: simulation sweep is the ground-truth harness; the same
  `estimate_angle()` call will consume live frames unchanged once 2-channel
  hardware is available.
